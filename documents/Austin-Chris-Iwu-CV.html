Earmark · Celo Agents at Work · Winner, Best Stablecoin Adoption
Sep 2026 · earmark-agent.onrender.com

Telegram agent on Celo that collects money for one shared bill, such as school fees, and can only pay the wallet the group locked in when the drive was created. Each share moves straight from the payer to the payee in a single transaction, so the contract never holds a balance and there is no treasurer to trust; the bot tracks who has paid and nudges the rest. Supports 25 Celo stablecoins, x402 payments from other agents, fee abstraction, and ERC-8004 agent identity. Live on Celo mainnet.
